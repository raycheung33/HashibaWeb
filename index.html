Hashiba

Your old Palm eBooks and iSilo documents, readable again on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone, looking the way they were made to look.

THE BOOKS YOU ALREADY HAVE

Hashiba opens .pdb files: iSilo documents made by older and newer versions of iSiloX, and PalmDOC eBooks. Add them from inside the app, or open one from Files or another app (“Open in” on iPhone and iPad, “Open with” on Android). Each book is copied into the app, so it stays in your library.

FORMATTING KEPT INTACT

  • Text styles: colours, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, superscript and subscript.
  • Layout: font sizes, paragraph alignment and spacing, and bulleted and nested lists.
  • Pictures shown where they sit in the book.
  • Tables, including nested tables and cell colours. Wide tables scroll sideways.

LINKS THAT WORK

  • Links inside a book jump straight to the right place.
  • Back and Forward take you through where you have been, and Home returns you to the start.
  • Web, email and phone links open in the app that handles them.

MARK, SEARCH AND ORGANISE

  • Bookmarks on any page, to jump straight back.
  • Highlights in several colours. Select text to highlight it or copy it.
  • Search the whole book, or only the section you are reading.
  • Tags on your books, to filter the library by them.
  • Export a book as a .hashiba file with its bookmarks and highlights, to keep or share. Open the file in Hashiba to bring them back.

COMFORTABLE READING

  • Swipe between pages, or turn off page mode and scroll.
  • Tap the page to hide the toolbars and read full screen.
  • Make the text bigger or smaller with A− and A+.
  • Dark mode, with the book’s colours adjusted to stay readable.
  • Every book reopens where you stopped reading.

CHINESE AND OTHER LANGUAGES

The text encoding is detected automatically. If a book looks garbled, choose another from the menu: UTF-8, Traditional Chinese (Big5), Simplified Chinese (GBK), Japanese (Shift_JIS) or Western (Windows-1252).

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account, no ads and no tracking. Hashiba never connects to the internet: your books stay on your device unless you choose to export or share one.
