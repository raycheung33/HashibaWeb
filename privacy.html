Privacy Policy

Hashiba · Last updated 30 October 2026

Hashiba is an app for iPhone, iPad and Android for reading Palm eBooks and iSilo .pdb documents. This page explains what the app stores, where it stays, and who else sees it. Hashiba is operated by Cheung Pak Wai, and “we” and “us” mean us.

The short version: we collect nothing. Hashiba never connects to the internet, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. On Android it does not even have permission to. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting inside the app.

What the app stores on your device

To work as a library, Hashiba keeps the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • Copies of the books you open. When you add a .pdb file from inside the app, or open one in Hashiba from Files or another app, the app copies it in, so the book keeps opening after a restart.
  • A list of those books: each one’s title, file size and the text encoding it uses.
  • What you add to each book: where you stopped reading, your bookmarks and highlights (with the highlighted text), any name you gave it and its tags.
  • Your settings: text size, dark mode and page mode.

Hashiba only sees the files you choose to open. It does not browse your storage, and it does not ask for access to your photos, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

What reaches us

Nothing. We run no server for Hashiba, and the app has no way to connect to one. We never see your books, your library or your reading.

When other apps or services are involved

  • Links in your books. If you tap a web, email or phone link inside a book, Hashiba hands it to the app on your device that handles it: your browser, mail app or dialler. What happens next is covered by that app’s privacy policy, not this one.
  • Exporting and sharing. When you export a book, Hashiba makes a .hashiba file holding the book and, if you choose, its bookmarks and highlights. When you copy text, it goes on your clipboard. Where a file or text goes after that, whether a folder, a message or another app, is your choice, and is covered by whatever you send it to.
  • iCloud and device backups. On iPhone and iPad, Hashiba’s storage, including the copied books, is included in your iCloud Backup or computer backup if you have one turned on. That is controlled by your device’s settings and Apple’s privacy policy, and the backup never reaches us.
  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may include Hashiba’s storage, including the copied books, in your device backup with Google. That is controlled by your phone’s settings and Google’s privacy policy, and the backup never reaches us.
  • The App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google handle downloading and updating the app. If you have chosen to share analytics or diagnostics with them, they may pass us anonymous statistics such as install counts and crash reports, which do not include your books or anything that identifies you.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising. We have none to sell or share.

Deleting your data

  • One book: in the library, swipe the book to the left and tap Delete (iPhone and iPad), or tap its ⋮ menu and tap Delete (Android). The app’s copy of the file is deleted along with its reading position, bookmarks, highlights and tags.
  • Everything: delete or uninstall Hashiba, or on Android clear its storage in the system settings. That deletes every copied book and every setting.

Removing a book from Hashiba never touches the original file you opened it from.

Children

Hashiba collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version handles your information differently, we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version is released.
